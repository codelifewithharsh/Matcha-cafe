## 3. Brand Identity & Aesthetics

### Vibe Keywords
`cozy` · `serene` · `quaint` · `charming` · `minimal` · `warm` · `Japanese-inspired` · `intimate` · `community-first`

### Visual Identity (inferred from reviews, photos, brand)
- **Color Palette (suggested):**
  - Primary: Matcha green — `#5C7A45` / `#7AAD5B`
  - Soft sakura pink — `#F2B8C6` / `#E8909F`
  - Warm off-white / cream — `#FAF6F0`
  - Deep charcoal for text — `#2B2B2B`
  - Accent: dusty rose — `#D4817A`
- **Aesthetic:** Warm minimalism. Japanese wabi-sabi meets Bangalore indie cafe culture.
- **Typography feel:** Elegant serif for headings (think editorial/Japanese calm). Clean sans-serif for body.
- **Photography style:** Natural sunlight, pottery mugs, pastel drinks, close-up textures.
- **Unique design detail:** Pottery mugs hand-crafted by one of the founders — this is a core brand story element.

### Brand Personality
- Artisanal, not corporate
- Calm and grounded, not loud
- Community-driven (Slow Sundays Matcha Club)
- Authentic (matcha sourced directly from Japan)
- Women-led with a personal story

## 8. Website Pages & Sections Recommended

### Pages
1. **Home** — Hero, tagline, brand story hook, featured drinks
2. **Our Story** — Founders story, from pop-up to store, the Japan sourcing, the pottery mugs
3. **Menu** — Full menu with categories (Matcha / Coffee / Food / Soft Serve)
4. **Community** — Slow Sundays Matcha Club, merch, events
5. **Visit Us** — Two locations with maps, hours
6. **Gallery** — Instagram-style photo grid

### Key Sections for Home
- Hero with a strong visual and tagline: *"Bengaluru's First Matcha Bar"*
- "Why Sakura?" — 3-4 USP cards (Japan-sourced, Women-founded, Handcrafted pottery, Ceremonial grade)
- Signature drink spotlight (OG Matcha Latte + Strawberry Matcha Latte)
- Community section — Slow Sundays CTA
- Instagram feed embed or static gallery
- Location cards for both outlets

## 9. Tone of Voice for Copy

- **Calm and warm** — like a conversation over matcha
- **Poetic but grounded** — not pretentious, not loud
- **Proud of craft** — mention sourcing, process, pottery with quiet confidence
- **Inclusive** — welcoming to matcha beginners, respectful of enthusiasts
- Use occasional Japanese words where tasteful (e.g., "Ocha" for tea, "Ichigo" for strawberry)
- Avoid: loud exclamation marks, overhyped marketing language, corporate-sounding copy

---

## 10. Technical Suggestions for Claude Code

- **Framework:** Next.js (SSG for performance) or plain HTML/CSS/JS
- **Fonts:** 
  - Display: Cormorant Garamond or Playfair Display (elegant serif)
  - Body: DM Sans or Outfit (clean, modern)
- **Animations:** Subtle scroll-reveal, soft fade-ins. No jarring transitions.
- **Mobile-first:** Core audience is young Bangalore crowd — heavy mobile usage
- **Color mode:** Light theme (cream/white base) with green + pink accents
- **Instagram embed:** Consider embedding @sakuramatchabar feed on home/gallery page
- **Google Maps embed:** For both locations
- **No e-commerce needed** (no online ordering as of now)
